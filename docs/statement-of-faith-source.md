## Statement of Faith

We Believe according to the Holy Bible that:

1. There is one God who is the Father, the Son Jesus Christ, and the Holy Spirit, in Whom we are one;

2. God alone is self-existing and eternal;

3. All Scripture is inspired by God, for men spoke as they were moved by the Holy Spirit;

4. All have sinned and come short of the glory of God;

5. When the appropriate time came, God sent His Son, born of a woman;

6. The Virgin Mary bore a Son who was conceived of the Holy Spirit;

7. The Son as the Word with God in the beginning was God; yet He became flesh and dwelt among us;

8. He was named Jesus because He saves His people from their sins;

9. God laid on Him the iniquity of us all, making Him sin on our behalf;

10. Christ was crucified for our sins, was buried and was raised the third day, according to the Scriptures;

11. All who confess Jesus as Lord and believe that God has raised Him from the dead, will be saved;

12. Jesus is coming again to judge the living and the dead and to receive the believers to Himself.
